WISH vs HOPE

"Wish" se usa para deseos irreales o improbables. "Hope" se usa para deseos posibles/futuros.

  • • I wish I were taller. (no soy alto - irreal)
  • • I hope you pass the exam. (es posible - real)

"If only" es similar a "wish" pero más enfático/dramático.

3 Tipos de Wish

  • 1. Wish + Past → presente
  • 2. Wish + Past Perfect → pasado
  • 3. Wish + would → futuro / queja

Tipo 1: WISH + Past Simple — Presente Irreal

Para hablar de algo que quisieras que fuera diferente AHORA.

Fórmula: I wish + Past Simple

  • I wish I knew the answer. (no sé)
  • I wish I had more money. (no tengo)
  • I wish you were here. (no estás)
  • She wishes she spoke Chinese. (no lo habla)

Nota: Igual que en el Second Conditional, usa "were" para todas las personas (formal).

Tipo 2: WISH + Past Perfect — Arrepentimiento Pasado

Para arrepentirte de algo que pasó (o no pasó) en el pasado.

Fórmula: I wish + Past Perfect (had + participle)

  • I wish I had studied harder. (no estudié)
  • I wish I hadn't said that. (sí lo dije)
  • She wishes she had taken the job. (no lo tomó)
  • We wish we hadn't sold the car. (sí lo vendimos)

Tipo 3: WISH + Would — Quejas / Futuro deseado

Para quejas sobre comportamientos de otros, o cosas molestas que pasan repetidamente.

Fórmula: I wish + would + verb

  • I wish you would stop shouting!
  • I wish it would stop raining.
  • She wishes her boss would listen to her.
  • I wish they would call back.

IMPORTANTE: No puedes usar "wish + would" con "I" o "we" (no puedes quejarte de ti mismo).
I wish I would study more. → ✅ I wish I studied more.

IF ONLY (versión enfática)

"If only" funciona igual que "wish" pero suena más dramático y emotivo.

Presente irreal

If only I knew!

If only I were rich!

Pasado / Arrepentimiento

If only I had listened!

If only she had stayed!

Queja / futuro

If only he would call!

If only it would snow!

HOPE — Para deseos posibles

Cuando el deseo es realista / posible, usa HOPE (no wish).

  • I hope (that) you pass the exam.
  • I hope (that) it doesn't rain tomorrow.
  • I hope (that) she will come.
  • We hope (that) you are doing well.

Nota: Después de "hope" puedes usar Present Simple para futuro: "I hope you have a great time" (no "had").
