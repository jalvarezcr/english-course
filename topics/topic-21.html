Domina los tiempos perfect continuous: duración hasta un punto.

¿Qué son los Perfect Continuous?

Los tiempos Perfect Continuous combinan dos ideas:

  • Perfect: conexión con otro tiempo
  • Continuous: acción en proceso

Resultado: hablan de la duración de una acción hasta un punto determinado.

Fórmula Universal

have/had/will have +

been + verb-ing

1. Present Perfect Continuous

Fórmula: have/has + been + verb-ing

Se usa para:

  • 1. Acciones que empezaron en el pasado y continúan ahora

    I have been studying English for 5 years.

  • 2. Acciones recientes con resultado visible AHORA

    She's tired because she has been running.

    Your hair is wet. Have you been swimming?

  • 3. Enfatizar la duración

    I have been waiting for an hour!

FormaEjemplo
✅I have been working all day.
❌She hasn't been sleeping well.
❓Have you been waiting long?

2. Past Perfect Continuous

Fórmula: had + been + verb-ing

Se usa para:

  • 1. Acción que continuaba antes de OTRO evento pasado

    She had been crying when I arrived.

    I had been waiting for 30 minutes before the bus came.

  • 2. Causa de un resultado en el pasado

    He was exhausted because he had been running.

    The streets were wet — it had been raining.

FormaEjemplo
✅They had been studying for hours.
❌She hadn't been feeling well.
❓How long had you been waiting?

3. Future Perfect Continuous

Fórmula: will have + been + verb-ing

Se usa para:

  • 1. Duración de una acción hasta un punto en el futuro

    By 2030, I will have been working here for 10 years.

    Next month, we will have been living together for 5 years.

  • 2. Predecir la causa de un futuro resultado

    She will be tired when she arrives — she will have been traveling for 12 hours.

FormaEjemplo
✅By June, I will have been studying for 3 years.
❌She won't have been working by then.
❓How long will you have been waiting?

Perfect SIMPLE vs Perfect CONTINUOUS

La diferencia es sutil pero importante: Simple se enfoca en el resultado/cantidad. Continuous se enfoca en la duración/proceso.

Present Perfect Simple — Resultado

  • I have written 3 emails. (cantidad)
  • She has read the book. (completado)
  • We have visited Paris. (experiencia)

Present Perfect Continuous — Duración

  • I have been writing for hours. (actividad)
  • She has been reading all morning. (en proceso)
  • It has been raining since 5 PM. (continuidad)

Compara:

• "I have read 3 books this month." → terminé 3 libros.

• "I have been reading all month." → he estado leyendo continuamente.

¡Cuidado! Verbos que NO se usan en Continuous

Igual que en el present continuous, los stative verbs no se usan en perfect continuous:

No digas:

  • I have been knowing him for years.
  • She has been loving this song.
  • We have been having a car since 2020.

Usa Perfect Simple:

  • I have known him for years. ✓
  • She has loved this song. ✓
  • We have had a car since 2020. ✓
