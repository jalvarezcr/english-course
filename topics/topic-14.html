He said, She said...

Aprende a contar chismes (y noticias) correctamente en inglés.

¿Qué es?

El Reported Speech (Discurso Indirecto) es lo que usamos cuando queremos contarle a alguien lo que otra persona dijo.

La regla de oro es: El tiempo retrocede. Como estamos contando algo que ya se dijo, movemos el verbo "un paso atrás" hacia el pasado.

El Efecto Eco

I am happy.

Directo (Ahora)

He said he was happy.

Indirecto (Pasado)

La Máquina del Tiempo (Backshift)

Mira cómo los tiempos verbales viajan al pasado cuando reportamos:

Present Simple

"I like pizza."

Past Simple

She said she liked pizza.

Present Continuous

"I am working."

Past Continuous

He said he was working.

Future (Will)

"I will call you."

Conditional (Would)

She said she would call me.

Modal (Can)

"I can swim."

Modal (Could)

He said he could swim.

¿Said o Told?

Use SAID when...

No dices a quién se lo dijo.

"He said (that) he was tired."

Use TOLD when...

Dices el nombre de la persona.

"He told me (that) he was tired."

Tabla Completa: "Retrocede un Tiempo"

La regla mágica del Reported Speech: cada tiempo retrocede un paso hacia el pasado.

Estilo Directo (lo dicho) → Estilo Reportado
Present Simple (work)→Past Simple (worked)
Present Continuous (am working)→Past Continuous (was working)
Past Simple (worked)→Past Perfect (had worked)
Present Perfect (have worked)→Past Perfect (had worked)
Will (will work)→Would (would work)
Can→Could
May / Must→Might / Had to
Going to→Was/were going to

Preguntas Reportadas (Reported Questions)

Cuando reportas una pregunta, la estructura cambia drásticamente: ya no es una pregunta, es una declaración.

WH- Questions (What, Where, Who...)

Estructura: ASKED + WH- + sujeto + verbo (sin auxiliar do/does)

  • "Where do you live?"
    → He asked where I lived.
  • "What time is it?"
    → She asked what time it was.
  • "Why did you leave?"
    → He asked why I had left.

Yes/No Questions

Estructura: ASKED + IF/WHETHER + sujeto + verbo

  • "Are you tired?"
    → She asked if I was tired.
  • "Do you like pizza?"
    → He asked if I liked pizza.
  • "Will you come?"
    → She asked whether I would come.

Cuidado: En reported questions NO se usa "do/does/did" y el orden es sujeto + verbo (NO verbo + sujeto). Y no lleva signo de interrogación al final.

Órdenes y Peticiones Reportadas

Para reportar órdenes, peticiones o consejos, usa: verbo de reporte + persona + TO + verbo.

TOLD (orden)

"Sit down!"
→ He told me to sit down.

ASKED (petición)

"Please help me."
→ She asked me to help her.

ADVISED (consejo)

"You should rest."
→ The doctor advised me to rest.

Para órdenes/peticiones negativas: agrega NOT antes de TO

  • "Don't be late!" → She told me not to be late.
  • "Please don't shout." → He asked me not to shout.
  • "Don't smoke here." → He warned me not to smoke there.

Excepciones: Cuándo NO retroceder

En algunos casos no retrocedes el tiempo. Memorízalos:

1. Verbo de reporte en PRESENTE

Si dices "He says", el tiempo NO cambia.

"I am tired." → He says (that) he is tired.

2. Verdades universales

Hechos eternos no cambian.

She said the sun rises in the east. (NO "rose")

3. Si la situación sigue siendo verdad

Si lo dicho aún es relevante ahora.

She said she is a teacher. (sigue siéndolo)

4. Modales que ya son pasado

would, could, should, might, must (probabilidad) NO cambian.

"You should sleep." → He said I should sleep.
