La Batalla de las Palabras

En inglés, la forma en que comparamos depende del tamaño de la palabra (sílabas).

  • Comparativo: 1 vs 1. (Yo soy más alto que tú).
    Palabra clave: THAN (que).
  • Superlativo: 1 vs Todos. (Yo soy el más alto del grupo).
    Palabra clave: THE (el/la).

Reglas Ortográficas (-ER / -EST)

Al agregar -er o -est, el verbo a veces cambia. Estas son las 4 reglas:

1. Regla general: + ER / + EST

  • tall → taller → the tallest
  • cheap → cheaper → the cheapest
  • young → younger → the youngest

2. Termina en E: + R / + ST

  • nice → nicer → the nicest
  • large → larger → the largest
  • safe → safer → the safest

3. CVC (corta): duplica consonante

  • big → bigger → the biggest
  • hot → hotter → the hottest
  • thin → thinner → the thinnest

4. Consonante + Y: cambia Y por IER/IEST

  • happy → happier → the happiest
  • easy → easier → the easiest
  • busy → busier → the busiest

Comparativos de Igualdad (AS ... AS)

Para decir que dos cosas son iguales, usamos la estructura: AS + adjetivo + AS ("tan ... como").

✅ Afirmativo (igual que)

  • Tom is as tall as Mike. → Tom es tan alto como Mike.
  • This book is as interesting as that one.
  • She runs as fast as her brother.

❌ Negativo (no tan ... como)

  • I'm not as tall as my dad.
  • This phone isn't as expensive as the new one.
  • The blue car isn't as fast as the red one.

LESS / THE LEAST (Menos / el menos)

Para comparar hacia abajo (al contrario de "more / the most").

LESS ... THAN (menos que)

  • This book is less interesting than that one.
  • Coffee is less sweet than juice.
  • I'm less tired than yesterday.

Se usa principalmente con adjetivos largos.

THE LEAST (el/la menos)

  • This is the least expensive car here.
  • Math is the least difficult subject for me.
  • He's the least talkative in the group.

Adjetivos de 2 Sílabas (Casos Especiales)

Los adjetivos de 2 sílabas son confusos. Sigue estas guías:

2 sílabas terminadas en -Y: usa -er/-est

  • happy → happier → the happiest
  • easy → easier → the easiest
  • noisy → noisier → the noisiest
  • funny → funnier → the funniest

2 sílabas terminadas en otras letras: usa more/most

  • modern → more modern → the most modern
  • famous → more famous → the most famous
  • boring → more boring → the most boring
  • useful → more useful → the most useful
