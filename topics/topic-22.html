By 2030, I will have...

Future Perfect, Future Continuous y los 6 modos de hablar del futuro.

El Futuro Tiene 6 Formas

Ya conoces WILL y GOING TO. Pero el inglés tiene 6 formas distintas de hablar del futuro, cada una con matiz único. En este tema dominas las más avanzadas.

6 Formas del Futuro

  • 1. Will
  • 2. Going to
  • 3. Present Continuous
  • 4. Present Simple
  • 5. Future Continuous
  • 6. Future Perfect

1. Future Continuous

Fórmula: will + be + verb-ing

Se usa para:

  • 1. Acciones en proceso en un momento futuro

    This time tomorrow, I will be flying to Tokyo.

    At 8 PM tonight, we will be watching the game.

  • 2. Eventos planeados (más educado que "will")

    I will be working late tonight.

    Will you be using the car later?

  • 3. Predicción sobre presente/futuro

    Don't call now — they will be having dinner.

FormaEjemplo
✅I will be working at 9 AM.
❌She won't be coming tonight.
❓Will you be using this?

2. Future Perfect

Fórmula: will + have + participle

Se usa para:

  • 1. Acciones que estarán COMPLETADAS antes de un futuro punto

    By 2030, I will have graduated.

    By the time you arrive, I will have finished dinner.

  • 2. Predicciones sobre el presente

    She will have arrived by now. (suposición)

Palabras clave: by + tiempo, by then, by the time, in 5 years

FormaEjemplo
✅By 5 PM, I will have finished.
❌She won't have completed it by then.
❓Will you have arrived by 8?

Future Continuous vs Future Perfect

Future Continuous (en proceso)

At 8 PM, I will be eating dinner.

→ En ese momento estaré cenando.

Future Perfect (terminado)

By 8 PM, I will have eaten dinner.

→ Antes de las 8 ya habré cenado.

Bonus: Future Perfect Continuous

Fórmula: will have been + verb-ing — Para enfatizar la duración hasta un punto futuro.

  • By next month, I will have been working here for 5 years.
  • By 2030, she will have been teaching for 20 years.
  • When you graduate, you will have been studying for 4 years.
