Actions Now

Happening Right Now!

Cómo describir lo que está pasando en este preciso momento.

¿Cuándo se usa?

Usamos el Present Continuous para acciones que están ocurriendo ahora mismo o alrededor de este momento.

La clave es el final -ING (que equivale a "-ando" o "-iendo" en español).

Diferencia Clave

I eat pizza.

Present Simple (Rutina)

I am eating pizza.

Present Continuous (¡Ahora!)

La Fórmula Mágica

Necesitas 3 ingredientes obligatorios:

Sujeto (She)
To Be (is)
Verbo + ING (working)
¡Nunca olvides el verbo TO BE! No digas "She working". Di "She is working".

Reglas de Ortografía

A veces el verbo cambia un poco antes de agregar ING.

1. La mayoría

Solo agrega -ing.

  • Work → Working
  • Play → Playing
  • Eat → Eating
2. Termina en 'E'

Elimina la 'e' y agrega -ing.

  • Dance → Dancing
  • Make → Making
  • Write → Writing
3. Sándwich (CVC)

Si es corto y termina en Consonante-Vocal-Consonante, duplica la última letra.

  • Run → Running
  • Swim → Swimming
  • Sit → Sitting

Negativo, Pregunta y Respuestas Cortas

Forma Negativa: Sujeto + TO BE + NOT + verbo-ING

Sin contracción

I am not sleeping.

She is not working.

They are not playing.

Con contracción (más común)

I'm not sleeping.

She isn't working.

They aren't playing.

Pregunta: TO BE + Sujeto + verbo-ING?

Am I dreaming? → ¿Estoy soñando?

Is she studying? → ¿Está estudiando?

Are they coming? → ¿Están viniendo?

What are you doing? → ¿Qué estás haciendo?

Why is he crying? → ¿Por qué está llorando?

Respuestas Cortas (Igual que TO BE)

Afirmativa (SIN contracción)

  • — Are you working? → Yes, I am.
  • — Is she eating? → Yes, she is.
  • — Are they playing? → Yes, they are.

Negativa (CON contracción)

  • — Are you sleeping? → No, I'm not.
  • — Is he running? → No, he isn't.
  • — Are we late? → No, we aren't.

Los 4 Usos del Present Continuous

No es solo para "ahora mismo". Tiene 4 funciones específicas:

1

Acciones en este momento

"I am writing an email right now."

"Look! The baby is laughing."

Palabras clave: now, right now, at the moment

2

Acciones temporales (around now)

"She is studying French this semester."

"I am reading a great book these days."

Palabras clave: today, this week, this month, these days

3

Planes futuros agendados

"I am meeting Carlos tomorrow at 5."

"We are traveling to Paris next month."

Palabras clave: tomorrow, next week, tonight + hora

4

Quejas con "always"

"He is always losing his keys!"

"You are always complaining!"

Tono: Frustración / irritación por costumbre molesta

⚠️ Verbos que NO usan -ING (Stative Verbs)

Algunos verbos expresan estados, emociones o pensamientos. Estos NO se usan en presente continuo, incluso si hablas de algo "ahora mismo".

Emociones / Gustos

  • love — amar
  • like — gustar
  • hate — odiar
  • prefer — preferir
  • want — querer
  • need — necesitar

Mentales / Opinión

  • know — saber
  • think (opinar)
  • believe — creer
  • understand — entender
  • remember — recordar
  • forget — olvidar

Sentidos / Posesión

  • have (tener)
  • own — poseer
  • see — ver
  • hear — oír
  • seem — parecer
  • be — ser/estar

Incorrecto

  • I am loving this song.
  • She is knowing the answer.
  • We are wanting pizza.

Correcto (usa Present Simple)

  • I love this song.
  • She knows the answer.
  • We want pizza.

Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

La confusión más grande de los estudiantes. Aquí la diferencia clara:

Aspecto Present SIMPLE Present CONTINUOUS
Uso Rutinas, hechos, gustos Ahora, temporal, futuro planeado
Ejemplo I work in a bank. I am working from home today.
Palabras always, usually, every day, often now, right now, today, at the moment
Forma Verbo (+S en he/she/it) am/is/are + verbo-ING

Compara estas dos oraciones:

• She speaks English. → (en general, ella habla inglés)

• She is speaking English (right now). → (en este momento)

Look! What are they doing?

They / Play

They are playing soccer.

He / Exercise

He is exercising.

She / Drink

She is drinking coffee.

I / Plan

I am planning my day.
