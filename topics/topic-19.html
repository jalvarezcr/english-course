¿Qué es el Second Conditional?

El Second Conditional se usa para hablar de situaciones hipotéticas, irreales o improbables en el presente o futuro. Es decir: cosas que NO van a pasar, pero que imaginas.

Recuerda: el First Conditional es real ("If it rains, I'll stay home"). El Second Conditional es hipotético ("If I had a million dollars, I would travel the world").

Fórmula Clave

If + Past Simple,

Subject + would + verb

Estructura Completa

El verbo en la condición va en pasado simple, pero el significado es del presente/futuro hipotético.

Condición (IF)

If + Past Simple

If I won the lottery,

Resultado

would + verb

I would buy a house.

Forma Ejemplo
✅ AfirmativoIf I had time, I would learn Italian.
❌ NegativoIf I didn't have a job, I wouldn't have money.
❓ PreguntaWhat would you do if you won?
📦 ContracciónI'd buy a car. / She wouldn't go.

Regla Especial: "If I WERE"

En el Second Conditional, con el verbo TO BE, lo correcto es usar WERE para TODAS las personas (es el subjuntivo).

✅ Correcto (formal)

  • If I were you, I would apologize.
  • If he were taller, he could play basketball.
  • If she were rich, she'd travel a lot.
  • If it were sunny, we'd go to the park.

⚠ Aceptable (informal)

  • If I was you... (común en habla diaria)
  • If he was here, he'd help.

En exámenes formales (TOEFL, IELTS, Cambridge): siempre usa WERE.

Frase clave: "If I were you..."

Es la forma más común y educada de dar consejos en inglés.

  • "If I were you, I would call him." (Yo en tu lugar lo llamaría.)
  • "If I were you, I'd take the job offer."

Los 4 Usos del Second Conditional

1

Situaciones imaginarias

"If I had wings, I would fly."

"If aliens existed, what would they look like?"

2

Situaciones improbables

"If I won the lottery, I would buy an island."

"If you met the president, what would you say?"

3

Dar consejos (formal)

"If I were you, I wouldn't do that."

"If I were in your shoes, I would speak to him."

4

Peticiones muy educadas

"Would you mind if I opened the window?"

"What would you say if I asked for a raise?"

First vs Second Conditional

La diferencia está en cuán probable consideras la situación.

Aspecto First Conditional Second Conditional
ProbabilidadReal, probableHipotético, improbable
EstructuraIf + Present, will + verbIf + Past, would + verb
EjemploIf it rains, I will stay home.If I were rich, I would travel.
SignificadoPuede que llueva (real)No soy rico (hipotético)

Compara estas dos:

• First: "If I see him, I will tell him." → Probablemente lo veré.

• Second: "If I saw him, I would tell him." → Probablemente no lo veré.

Alternativas a WOULD

Puedes reemplazar WOULD con otros modales para variar el significado:

COULD (posibilidad)

If I had more time, I could finish it.

MIGHT (incertidumbre)

If we left now, we might catch the train.
