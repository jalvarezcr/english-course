Acción Terminada

El Pasado Simple se usa para acciones que ya terminaron. No tienen conexión con el presente. Es como cerrar un libro.

En inglés, tenemos dos tipos de verbos en pasado: los que siguen las reglas (Regulares) y los rebeldes (Irregulares).

Palabras Clave

  • Yesterday (Ayer)
  • Last week (La semana pasada)
  • 2 days ago (Hace 2 días)
  • In 1999

Verbos Regulares (+ED)

La mayoría de los verbos son "buenos ciudadanos" y solo necesitan -ed al final.

Regla General

Agrega -ed

Work → Worked

Play → Played

Visit → Visited

Termina en 'E'

Solo agrega -d

Live → Lived

Dance → Danced

Love → Loved

Termina en Consonante + Y

Cambia Y por I + ed

Study → Studied

Cry → Cried

Try → Tried

Regla Extra: Verbos Cortos (CVC)

Si el verbo es corto (1 sílaba) y termina en Consonante-Vocal-Consonante, duplica la última letra antes de -ed.

Doble la consonante final

  • stop → stopped
  • plan → planned
  • chat → chatted
  • fit → fitted
  • drop → dropped

NO doblar (terminan en W, X, Y)

  • play → played (no playyed)
  • show → showed
  • fix → fixed
  • visit → visited (2 sílabas, sin estrés final)

Pronunciación del -ED (¡Importante!)

La terminación -ed se pronuncia de 3 formas distintas según el sonido final del verbo.

/t/ — "t"

Cuando el verbo termina en sonido sordo (k, p, s, ch, sh, f, x).

  • worked → /workt/
  • walked → /walkt/
  • watched → /watcht/
  • kissed → /kisst/
  • helped → /helpt/

/d/ — "d"

Cuando el verbo termina en sonido sonoro (vocales y la mayoría de consonantes).

  • played → /pleid/
  • called → /kald/
  • opened → /oupand/
  • cleaned → /kliind/
  • loved → /lavd/

/ɪd/ — "id"

Cuando el verbo termina en T o D. Crea una sílaba extra.

  • wanted → /wantid/
  • visited → /vizitid/
  • needed → /niidid/
  • started → /staartid/
  • decided → /disaididid/

Truco: Pon tu mano en la garganta. Si vibra al pronunciar la última letra del verbo en infinitivo, es /d/. Si no vibra, es /t/.

El Pasado del Verbo TO BE: WAS / WERE

El verbo TO BE tiene su propia conjugación en pasado y NO necesita "did".

WAS

Para I, He, She, It (singular).

  • I was at home.
  • He was happy.
  • She was a doctor.
  • It was cold.

WERE

Para You, We, They (plural y "you").

  • You were late.
  • We were friends.
  • They were tired.
  • The kids were playing.
Forma I / He / She / It You / We / They
✅ AfirmativoI was happy.They were happy.
❌ NegativoI wasn't happy.They weren't happy.
❓ PreguntaWas I happy?Were they happy?
↩ RespuestaYes, I was. / No, I wasn't.Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.

Expresiones de Tiempo en Pasado

Estas palabras te ayudan a indicar cuándo pasó algo. Suelen ir al final de la oración.

Días/Momentos específicos

  • yesterday — ayer
  • this morning — esta mañana
  • last night — anoche
  • at 3 PM — a las 3
  • on Monday — el lunes

Con "LAST" (pasado/a)

  • last week — la semana pasada
  • last month — el mes pasado
  • last year — el año pasado
  • last summer — el verano pasado
  • last Monday — el lunes pasado

Con "AGO" (hace...)

  • 5 minutes ago
  • 2 hours ago
  • 3 days ago
  • a week ago
  • 10 years ago

"ago" va siempre después del periodo.

Ejemplos completos:

  • • I visited my grandmother last weekend.
  • • They moved to Mexico 3 years ago.
  • • She called me yesterday at 8 PM.
  • • We met last summer in Cancún.
DID

El Ladrón del Pasado

Cuando usamos DID (en preguntas) o DIDN'T (en negativos), este auxiliar "roba" el pasado del verbo principal.

Afirmativo: I worked yesterday.
Negativo: I didn't work yesterday.
Pregunta: Did you work yesterday?

Nota cómo "work" pierde la -ed cuando aparece "did".

Preguntas con WH- en Pasado

Estructura: WH- + DID + sujeto + verbo en infinitivo + ...?

WHAT

  • What did you do yesterday?
  • What did she eat?
  • → I worked.

WHERE

  • Where did you go last weekend?
  • Where did they meet?
  • → I went to Mexico City.

WHEN

  • When did you arrive?
  • When did he call?
  • → At 8 PM.

WHY

  • Why did you leave?
  • Why did she cry?
  • → Because I was tired.

WHO

  • Who did you see?
  • Who did they invite?
  • → I saw Maria.

HOW

  • How did you get there?
  • How did she learn English?
  • → By bus.

Recuerda: Con WAS/WERE no se usa "did". Pregunta directamente: "Where were you?" / "Was she at home?"

Respuestas Cortas en Pasado

Igual que en presente, no se contesta solo "Yes" o "No".

✅ Afirmativas

  • — Did you go to the party? → Yes, I did.
  • — Did she finish the project? → Yes, she did.
  • — Were you tired? → Yes, I was.
  • — Were they at home? → Yes, they were.

❌ Negativas

  • — Did you eat lunch? → No, I didn't.
  • — Did he call? → No, he didn't.
  • — Was it cold? → No, it wasn't.
  • — Were we late? → No, we weren't.

Historia Modelo: "Last weekend"

Lee este texto. Identifica los verbos regulares (verde), irregulares (ámbar) y "was/were" (violeta).

Last Saturday, I wanted to relax, so I went to the beach with my friends. We drove for two hours and we got there at 11 AM. The weather was beautiful and the water was warm.

We played volleyball, we swam in the ocean and we enjoyed the sun. At lunchtime, we ate tacos and we drank fresh lemonade. My friend Carlos brought a guitar and he sang some songs.

In the evening, we decided to leave. We were very tired but happy. I slept like a baby that night!

verde = verbos regulares (-ed)
ámbar = verbos irregulares
violeta = was / were
