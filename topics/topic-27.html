Cuando alguien más hace algo POR ti.

¿Qué es el Causative?

El causative es la forma de decir que contratas, pagas o pides a alguien para que haga algo POR ti.

Diferencia importante:

  • • "I cut my hair" → tú mismo te lo cortaste.
  • • "I had my hair cut" → un peluquero te lo cortó.

Fórmula

have/get + objeto + participio

have my car fixed

Forma 1: HAVE + OBJ + Past Participle

Es la forma más formal y común.

Ejemplos en diferentes tiempos:

  • Presente: I have my hair cut every month.
  • Pasado: She had her car repaired last week.
  • Futuro: We will have the house painted next month.
  • Modal: You should have your eyes checked.
  • Present Perfect: I have just had my teeth cleaned.
  • Pregunta: Where do you have your hair done?

Forma 2: GET + OBJ + Past Participle

Es más informal. Significa lo mismo que "have something done".

  • I got my phone fixed yesterday.
  • She wants to get her ears pierced.
  • We need to get the windows cleaned.
  • Let's get the documents translated tomorrow.
  • When did you get your nails done?

Tip: En conversación coloquial, "get" es más común. En texto formal, "have".

Causative Negativo (Experiencias malas)

También se usa para describir cuando algo malo te fue hecho (sin tu consentimiento).

  • I had my wallet stolen on the bus. (me robaron la cartera)
  • She had her bag snatched.
  • They had their house broken into.
  • He got his nose broken in the fight.

No significa que tú lo causaste — significa que te lo hicieron.

Causative con persona: HAVE / MAKE / LET / GET someone DO

Cuando "causas" que alguien específico haga algo.

VerboSignificadoEstructuraEjemplo
HAVEPedir (neutral)have + persona + verb (sin to)I had John fix the car.
MAKEObligarmake + persona + verb (sin to)She made me apologize.
LETPermitirlet + persona + verb (sin to)My parents let me drive.
GETPersuadir / convencerget + persona + TO + verbI got him to help me.

CUIDADO: Con HAVE/MAKE/LET el verbo va SIN "to". Pero con GET el verbo SÍ lleva "to".

Active vs Causative

Voz Activa (lo hago yo)

  • I cut my hair.
  • I fixed my car.
  • I painted my house.
  • I cleaned my suit.

Causative (alguien lo hace por mí)

  • I had my hair cut.
  • I had my car fixed.
  • I had my house painted.
  • I got my suit cleaned.
